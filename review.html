What this is, and isn't

  • From recordings: each district's count and NYT's calls (state pages, about every 10 minutes), and Kalshi's bid and ask for both sides (minute by minute).
  • All night (recomputed): the live job's own district-total projection, run on each moment's count from the first poll close. No later information goes in. The page itself didn't exist before 10:28 pm ET and had no Kalshi until 11:07 pm, so this is what those rules would have said.
  • As shown: the chance the page actually published: district totals until 12:08 am ET, county by county after that. The job was down from 5:48 am to 2:14 pm ET on Nov 4, so nothing new appears then. Its FLIP guard is approximated with the margin so far, and that guard changed during the night, so this isn't the page row for row.
  • Not here: county-by-county projections before they ran. The only county path for those hours is built from final results, which would leak the answer.
  • Fills are optimistic: every signal is filled in full at the ask, ignoring book depth. One night, and races moved together, so these groups are far from independent.
